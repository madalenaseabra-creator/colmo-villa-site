The House

Under the reed.

Colmo means thatch.

The roof of Comporta's old fishermen's huts, taken up in a contemporary house. Seven bedrooms in the main house for 14, plus an independent studio annex for 2. Light through the trees, cool floors, rooms that open to one another and close when you need them to.
