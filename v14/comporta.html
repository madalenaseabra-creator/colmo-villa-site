Comporta: beaches, rice fields and the village

Beaches, rice fields, the village, restaurants.

COLMO VILLA is in the Comporta Retreat, on the Alentejo coast. About 5 min to the beach · about 1h15 from Lisbon.

The beaches

The Atlantic is close — about 5 min to the beach. Comporta, Carvalhal, Pego and Melides follow the shore from north to south.

The rice fields

Rice fields are part of the landscape of the region, between the pines and the water.

The village

Comporta village is the slow centre of the day: a few streets, shops, and somewhere to sit.

Restaurants

There are restaurants in the village and around the area. Ask us for the ones we like when you write for dates.
