Our Story

The refuge of simplicity.

“Nothing was bought online.”

A house for friends, and for anyone who needs to stop. Under the reed, between the pines and the Atlantic. The pool, the gym, the sauna and the cinema are there when you want them — proof of a life lived slowly, not a list of features.

The way it was made

Portuguese tradition, held lightly.

What is natural, and lasts.

Time, and the quiet it needs. The materials of the place, the roof of the old huts, a contemporary house that does not raise its voice.
