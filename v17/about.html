About

Colmo means thatch.

The name says it: COLMO VILLA is three houses under thatched roofs, set among the pines of the Comporta Retreat, a gated estate in Carvalhal, on the Alentejo coast. The beach is five minutes away, and Lisbon about an hour and a quarter.

It was made for family and friends, and for anyone who needs to stop. Here the days have no plan unless you want one. Lunch at the long table runs into the afternoon, someone falls asleep over a book, someone else goes back to the pool, and in the evening everyone ends up around the fire. And there is a table big enough to bring everyone back together.

“Nothing was bought online.”
The owners, to NiT

The house was furnished slowly, with pieces found on family travels and at fairs. Every room holds something with a story. Go on holiday and stay right here, with daily housekeeping included.
