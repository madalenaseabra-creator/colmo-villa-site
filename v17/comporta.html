Comporta

Pines behind, Atlantic ahead.

COLMO VILLA sits in the Comporta Retreat, in Carvalhal, on the Alentejo coast, about an hour and a quarter from Lisbon. Behind the house are the pines, and five minutes ahead is the Atlantic, one long shore that runs from Comporta through Carvalhal and Pego down to Melides. Further along, the beaches grow quieter.

For lunch on the sand, we go to Sal, on Carvalhal beach, for fresh fish and seafood. In the evening, Jacaré, in Comporta village, cooks Brazilian and Italian, from pizza to risotto. And for rice the way it is made here, with fish and seafood, there is Dona Bia, in Torre, on the road between Comporta and Carvalhal, a classic of the area.

Between meals there is plenty to do, or nothing. Cavalos na Areia, in Torre, rides from the rice fields through the pines and dunes to the beach. Surf in Comporta teaches at Carvalhal beach, for every age, and takes paddleboards out on the Melides lagoon. The house bicycles are enough for the sandy paths and the rice fields, and at sunset the old stilt pier at Carrasqueira, over the Sado estuary, is worth the drive. Comporta village has a few streets of shops and somewhere to sit for a while.
