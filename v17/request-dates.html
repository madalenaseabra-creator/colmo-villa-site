FAQ

How many guests can stay?

Up to 16, in six en-suite bedrooms across three houses: four in Quiet Thatch, two in Studio Thatch.

Is cleaning included?

Yes. Daily housekeeping, 4 hours, is included, except on Sundays and public holidays.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 16:00 to 23:00. Check-out by 11:00.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes, pets are welcome. Tell us about them when you write for dates.

Is the house good for children?

There is a bunk room for six.
