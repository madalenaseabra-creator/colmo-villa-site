A little time for everything.

There is no programme at COLMO VILLA. A day here can be full from the first coffee to the last film, or it can be nothing at all, and the house is made for both. This is one day, hour by hour. Take all of it, or none.

08h

Coffee at the kitchen island while the house wakes up slowly. Or stay in bed a little longer.

11h

A swim, then a sunbed, then perhaps another swim. Nobody is counting.

13h

Lunch at the long table, which has a way of lasting until the afternoon.

15h

The bicycles are waiting, and the paths go into the pines. Or nowhere at all.

16h

A book in the library corner, and the same page for an hour.

18h

The sauna and the steam room, before the evening begins.

20h

Dinner, and then the fire, and the conversation that comes with it.

22h

A film in the cinema, or one more glass outside.
