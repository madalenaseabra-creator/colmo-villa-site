About

Colmo means thatch.

The name says it: COLMO VILLA is three houses under thatched roofs, set among the pines of the Comporta Retreat, a gated estate in Carvalhal, on the Alentejo coast. The beach is five minutes away, and Lisbon about an hour and a quarter.

It was made for family and friends, and for anyone who needs to stop. Here the days have no plan unless you want one. Lunch at the long table runs into the afternoon, someone falls asleep over a book, someone else goes back to the pool, and in the evening everyone ends up around the fire. There is room for everyone, and a table big enough to bring them all back together.

“Nothing was bought online.”
The owners, to NiT

The house was furnished slowly, with pieces found on family travels and at fairs. As the owners told NiT, “nothing was bought online”, and every room holds something with a story. Go on holiday and stay right here: the whole villa is yours, for your group only, with daily housekeeping included.
